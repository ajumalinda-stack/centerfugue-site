Some things are not meant to be handled internally.

When you need someone outside the system: someone who can listen without judgment, ask what people won't ask, go where you don't want to go, represent you without becoming you, tell you what they actually see, and when necessary, make the thing happen.

You don't need another consultant. You need someone you trust.

Give me the problem you can't say out loud to anyone who works for you, with you, or above you.

The more power you have, the fewer directions are safe to talk. Down changes how your team sees you. Sideways turns political. Up reads as losing control.

I'm the direction that's left.

I take full ownership, work out what needs to happen, and bring it back resolved. Nothing briefed to your team. No one else on a need-to-know basis but you.

"You're the one everyone brings their problems to. This is for the one you can't bring to anyone."
Who's on the other end

I'm Linda. For close to two decades I've worked inside large organizations, usually near the people carrying the most. I've watched what happens when someone runs out of safe people to tell, and built this to be that direction.

I have no stake in your organization, your outcome, or your reputation. That absence is the entire point.

Signature engagement

Represent my interests without representing my office.

Some situations need someone to go first: to test the water, reopen a door, or find out what's actually possible, before you're ready to put your name, your title, or your company on it.

I go in as myself: a trusted independent party, working on someone's behalf. I don't pretend to be you, and I don't lie about why I'm there. I just don't say whose interests I'm carrying until you're ready for that to be known.

  • "I need to know if they'd even consider this." Asked before it becomes an official ask.
  • "I want to reopen this relationship." Handled without you making the first move.
  • "Find out what they're actually asking for." Learned before you respond to what they said.
  • "Someone needs to talk to them who isn't my lawyer." Kept informal, outside official channels.

I can go where your title can't, carrying none of the institutional baggage a title brings with it.
